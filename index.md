## 📖 How to Use Zusia

### Asking Questions
While reading any PDF in Zotero, simply highlight text or type your question in the Zusia panel. Ask things like:
- "Explain this paragraph in simple terms"
- "What is the main argument of this section?"
- "How does Figure 3 relate to the results?"

### Working with LaTeX
Type mathematical expressions directly in the Zusia chat, or ask it to convert text into LaTeX. For example: "Write the quadratic formula in LaTeX" or "What does this equation mean?"

### Creating Drawings
Request visual explanations by saying "Draw a diagram of..." or "Create a flowchart showing..." Zusia will generate helpful visuals to accompany your reading.

### Exploring Theorem Links
When Zusia detects references to other theorems, it will show connection cards. Click these to see how ideas build on each other across papers.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Zusia won't appear in Zotero?**
Make sure you have Zotero 7 installed and that you installed the plugin correctly through Tools → Plugins.

**AI features not working?**
Check your internet connection and ensure you have an active account with the AI service you're using (Claude Code, Codex, or Antigravity).

**Zusia seems slow?**
Try closing other heavy applications and ensure your computer meets the minimum requirements.
